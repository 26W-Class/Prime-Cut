Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before your visit.

Do you accept walk-in clients without an appointment?

Yes. We reserve daily walk-in slots on a first-come, first-served basis, though booking ahead guarantees your preferred barber and time.

Should I wash my hair before arriving for my appointment?

If you use heavy pomades or clays, arriving with clean hair helps us assess natural fall—however, an invigorating wash is included with every Sovereign Cut and Executive Package.

Can we book the lounge privately for a wedding or corporate event?

Yes. Private half-day and full-lounge buyouts for groomsmen or executive teams are available Tuesday through Saturday. Choose "Private Events" in the form above or call our front desk directly.
