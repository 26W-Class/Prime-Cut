Established 2018 • Nairobi

Where Traditional Craft Meets Contemporary Calm

Crown & Blade was founded to elevate men’s grooming from a rushed errand into an intentional ritual—combining bespoke tailoring, warm hospitality, and unhurried precision.

The Story

Built for the Discerning Gentleman

Starting with two vintage hydraulic chairs in 2018, Master Barber Marcus Vance set out to create a lounge that honors old-world wet-shaving heritage while mastering modern hair textures and contemporary fades. Today, our four-chair flagship on Riverside Drive serves executives, creatives, and regulars who value consistency and quiet luxury.

Our Three Pillars

The principles that guide every appointment from the moment you step through our doors.

01 • Precision

Architectural Tailoring

No two head shapes or hair growth patterns are identical. We analyze crown swirl, density, and facial proportions before picking up a pair of shears.

02 • Hygiene

Clinical-Grade Sanitation

Every straight razor receives a fresh disposable blade in front of you. Combs, guards, and shears undergo hospital-grade UV and Barbicide sterilization after every client.

03 • Hospitality

Unhurried Lounge Atmosphere

We book generous 45-to-75 minute windows so you never feel rushed—complete with curated vinyl jazz, hot eucalyptus towels, and single-origin Kenyan espresso.
